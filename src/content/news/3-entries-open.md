Submissions are now open for Season 03 of the Pazhassi Short Film Festival. Independent filmmakers with films completed between 01 September 2023 and 31 August 2026 with runtimes under 30 minutes are invited to apply.

Awards will be contested across Best Short Film, Best Director, Best Cinematographer, and Best Popular Short Film. All submissions must be received before the deadline on 30 September 2026.
