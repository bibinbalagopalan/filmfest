The official web platform for the 3rd Pazhassi Short Film Festival has launched, engineered and designed by Bibin Balagopal. Marking a major digital milestone for Season 03, the entire festival intake pipeline is now fully online.

Filmmakers can complete registrations, upload screening materials, and track status digitally. Submissions will be securely streamed and evaluated directly by the evaluation committee from the comfort of their homes and dedicated devices, enabling an efficient and seamless curation process leading up to the final November screenings in Mananthavady.
