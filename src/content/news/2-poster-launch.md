The organizing committee has unveiled the official poster and visual identity for the 3rd Pazhassi Short Film Festival. Emphasizing the cultural legacy of Mananthavady and the raw ethos of independent short filmmaking, the new design establishes the graphic tone for Season 03.

The unveiling marks the formal commencement of festival season activities led by Drishtidosham Chalachithravedi and Pazhassi Raja Smaraka Granthalayam in collaboration with the Mananthavady Municipality.
