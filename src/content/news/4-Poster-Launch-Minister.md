The official brochure for the upcoming Pazhassi Short Film Fest was officially unveiled by the Honorable Minister for Culture, Cinema, and Tourism, Government of Kerala, Shri P.C. Vishnunadh.

The launch ceremony took place in the presence of Smt. Usha Vijayan, the Honorable Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) representing the Mananthavady constituency, who extended her support for the cultural initiative.

The short film festival is being organized in association with the Pazhassi Smaraka Granthalayam. Key office bearers of the library, including President Arun E.V. and Secretary Prasad V.K., were present at the event to mark the occasion.
